JotColoring product language — American English

Write for someone choosing, printing, or coloring a page. Use familiar words,
short instructions, active verbs, and friendly encouragement. Keep playful brand
lines such as “Pick a page. Make it yours.” but avoid a slogan where a clear action
or explanation is needed. Name the action: Search Pages, Download PDF, Image,
Open Coloring Studio, Save, Share, and Back up library.

Use color, coloring, favorite, center, gray, organize, canceled, and license.
Use kids, teens, and adults in context; do not use 18+ for drawing difficulty.
“Photo to Coloring Page” names the tool; “turn a photo into a coloring page”
explains the task. Use “coloring pages for adults,” not ambiguous adult-content
labels. Prefer sentence case for general UI; preserve accepted artwork titles.
Use singular topic modifiers: animal coloring page, flower coloring pages,
holiday coloring pages. Navigation category names remain plural where natural.

Describe visible artwork specifically. Do not infer unseen details from a title,
filename, or prompt. Keep headings, alt text, descriptions, social cards, and
search identity consistent through the shared content resolvers. Refresh
source-bound reviews when the image changes. Do not pad copy with keywords,
repeat a generic download sentence under every image, or promise rankings.

Image is the public button label; the actual extension and MIME remain truthful.
Current catalog PDFs use A4. American English does not authorize relabeling them
as Letter. Use en-US number/date formatting where a locale is specified. Keep
file bytes, limits, checksums, URLs, IDs, API states, and search synonyms unchanged
when editing prose. Internal state “cancelled” and accepted query “colouring”
are compatibility values, not public spelling errors.

Explain local saves plainly. Do not imply account sync, automatic public posting,
paid AI access, guaranteed photo likeness, guaranteed age suitability, or legal
rights beyond the implemented service. Legal meaning, permission terms, and
privacy behavior require careful separate review; a language edit must not
silently alter them. Avoid developer-only environment names in everyday help.

After copy changes, regenerate affected social covers; verify titles/descriptions,
links, and open dialogs, then check layout at 320/390/768/1024/1440 CSS pixels.
The style rules apply to new pages and imports as well as existing content.
