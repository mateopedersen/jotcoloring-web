# Verified subject emblems beside coloring previews

Specific brand and franchise cards use an explicitly reviewed local logo or official subject symbol alongside the existing JotColoring artwork preview. Unbranded subjects retain their topic icon. Never infer a logo from a parent category or similar subject name.

`src/content/subject-logos.json` records exact subject IDs, primary identity references, actual asset sources, file hashes, source hashes for rendered derivatives, and display contrast. Some official toy navigation assets depict the character rather than a standalone wordmark; their `asset_kind` records this distinction. Preserve supplied JotColoring artwork and its logo. NBA assets and their original thirty exact team mappings remain in their separate reviewed registry.

`src/content/subject-logo-reviews.json` binds the actual desktop/mobile card review to the asset hash, public asset path, display surface, shape and identity reference. It also identifies the real public drawing preview used in review. A replacement invalidates a mismatched review in the shared resolver; missing or unreviewed subjects use their existing generic topic icon. Use content-hashed asset filenames to avoid stale browser logos after a replacement.

For additions, retrieve an authentic asset from its official source, or verify an accurately attributed historical mark against an authoritative historical source. Inspect actual pixels and distinguish manufacturer marks from parent-company, model, award, dealer or unrelated site symbols. Preserve original retrieved bytes privately when a normalized derivative is needed; never draw or generate a substitute logo. Inspect final desktop and mobile cards with the matching actual public coloring preview and real counts. Run the logo integrity tests, normal web release checks and public file/card verification. Update the source-bound review only after completing these checks. Do not change catalog artwork, metadata, URLs, counts, import bounds or other categories to publish a card emblem.

This initial extension covers 35 automotive brands and 28 other branded subjects, in addition to the existing 30 NBA team logos. It does not claim that every remaining candidate is verified. Candidate references, rejected selections and the unfinished inventory remain in the private task work area until individually verified.

Additional source-reviewed batch: Hummer, Oldsmobile, Pontiac, Saturn and Saab use native GM discontinued-brand assets; X and TikTok use their official brand packs. All seven retain their actual live coloring previews and were inspected in desktop and mobile cards before activation. Source hashes, archive receipts where applicable, and final card proof hashes are recorded with each entry.
