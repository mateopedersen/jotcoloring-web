# Studio v4: 130 working features and controls

These are implemented controls and workflows, rather than 130 independent tools. The final browser audit exercises representative combinations; unit tests verify rendering and selection behavior.

1. **Textured pencil** — A fine grain for controlled shading. (`brush-pencil`)
2. **Wax crayon** — A broken wax texture instead of a flat digital stroke. (`brush-crayon`)
3. **Highlight marker** — Transparent multiply strokes for layering colors. (`brush-marker`)
4. **Watercolor wash** — Soft overlapping washes that build gradually. (`brush-watercolor`)
5. **Airbrush** — Feathered color for shadows and soft transitions. (`brush-airbrush`)
6. **Pen pressure** — Stroke width follows a compatible stylus. (`pressure`)
7. **Stroke stabilizer** — Adjust smoothing for steadier lines. (`stabilizer`)
8. **Stay inside an area** — Brush only inside the region selected with Fill. (`region-clip`)
9. **Pen-only painting** — Touch can navigate without leaving accidental paint. (`pen-only`)
10. **Straight line** — Drag a perfectly straight line. (`line`)
11. **Rectangle tool** — Draw borders, boxes, and blocks of color. (`rectangle`)
12. **Ellipse tool** — Add circles, ovals, and rounded details. (`ellipse`)
13. **Star tool** — Add scalable five-point stars. (`star`)
14. **Heart tool** — Add scalable heart shapes. (`heart`)
15. **Solid or outline shapes** — Choose a filled shape or a colorable border. (`shape-fill`)
16. **Constrained shapes** — Hold Shift for a square or circle. (`shape-constrain`)
17. **Left/right symmetry** — Paint both sides of a page together. (`symmetry-horizontal`)
18. **Top/bottom symmetry** — Reflect strokes across the horizontal center. (`symmetry-vertical`)
19. **Four-way reflection** — Mirror a stroke into all four corners. (`symmetry-both`)
20. **Four-part radial drawing** — Create a rotational repeating design. (`symmetry-radial4`)
21. **Eight-part radial drawing** — Build richer mandala-like patterns. (`symmetry-radial8`)
22. **Gradient fill** — Blend two chosen colors across a selected area. (`fill-linear`)
23. **Radial glow** — Blend from the center of an enclosed area. (`fill-radial`)
24. **Striped fill** — Fill a region with diagonal stripes. (`fill-stripes`)
25. **Polka-dot fill** — Add dots without crossing the selected outline. (`fill-dots`)
26. **Checkered fill** — Add a two-color checker pattern. (`fill-checks`)
27. **Pattern scale and gradient direction** — Control the size of a pattern and direction of a blend. (`fill-scale`)
28. **Curated color stories** — Eight ready-to-use coordinated palettes. (`palettes`)
29. **Color harmony generator** — Analogous, complementary, triadic, and tonal suggestions. (`harmonies`)
30. **Recent colors** — Return to the last 16 colors used. (`recent-colors`)
31. **My swatches** — Keep a personal palette with the saved project. (`saved-swatches`)
32. **Exact HEX entry** — Paste a precise color value. (`hex`)
33. **Palette from a photo** — Extract dominant colors from a private reference image. (`palette-extract`)
34. **Replace a layer color** — Recolor every matching mark on the active layer. (`replace-layer`)
35. **Replace across artwork** — Change matching paint, text, and object colors together. (`replace-all`)
36. **Named paint layers** — Keep details and backgrounds easy to identify. (`layer-rename`)
37. **Duplicate a paint layer** — Try a variation without losing the original layer. (`layer-duplicate`)
38. **Lock a paint layer** — Protect finished painting from accidental changes. (`layer-lock`)
39. **Layer opacity** — Fade a whole layer without changing its marks. (`layer-opacity`)
40. **Layer blending** — Normal, multiply, screen, and overlay. (`layer-blend`)
41. **Solo a layer** — Inspect one paint layer, then restore visibility. (`layer-solo`)
42. **Clear the active layer** — Start that layer again, with Undo available. (`layer-clear`)
43. **Remove a paint layer** — Remove an unwanted layer, with Undo available. (`layer-delete`)
44. **Live layer preview** — See the active layer without the rest of the artwork. (`layer-thumbnail`)
45. **Paper color** — Choose a background that exports with the artwork. (`paper-color`)
46. **Outline strength** — Soften or strengthen the original line art. (`line-opacity`)
47. **Canvas grid** — An alignment grid that stays out of downloads. (`grid`)
48. **Reference board** — Keep your own image beside the canvas, on this device. (`reference`)
49. **Compare original** — Hold a button to compare the uncolored outline. (`original`)
50. **Focus view** — Hide side panels while you color. (`focus`)
51. **Left-handed layout** — Move the tool and settings panels to the other side. (`left-handed`)
52. **Tool shortcuts** — Switch tools, zoom, fit, and pan from the keyboard. (`shortcuts`)
53. **Visual undo history** — Jump back to an earlier saved drawing state. (`history`)
54. **Named checkpoints** — Save up to eight named versions inside a project. (`versions`)
55. **Restore a checkpoint** — Return to a version with the current work kept in Undo. (`restore-version`)
56. **Rename in Studio** — Name your artwork without leaving the canvas. (`project-name`)
57. **Text font styles** — Choose sans serif, serif, rounded, or handwritten lettering. (`text-font`)
58. **Bold and italic text** — Give a caption more emphasis. (`text-weight`)
59. **Colorable text** — Turn lettering into an outline you can color. (`text-outline`)
60. **New canvas sizes** — Portrait, landscape, and square canvases. (`blank-presets`)
61. **Download without leaving** — Export from a preview dialog in Studio. (`export-panel`)
62. **PNG download** — Lossless colored artwork. (`export-png`)
63. **JPG download** — A compact full-color image. (`export-jpg`)
64. **JPEG download** — The same standard with the .jpeg extension. (`export-jpeg`)
65. **SVG download** — A self-contained SVG with lossless embedded artwork. (`export-svg`)
66. **Word download** — A real .docx document containing the colored artwork. (`export-word`)
67. **Image output size** — Original resolution or a smaller sharing copy. (`export-size`)
68. **Image quality control** — Choose a useful JPEG/WEBP size-quality balance. (`export-quality`)
69. **All-format download pack** — PNG, SVG, JPG, JPEG, Word, PDF, and WEBP together. (`export-bundle`)
70. **Editable backup from Studio** — Save a portable project backup with its history. (`local-backup`)
71. **Easy mode** — A simpler set of tools with automatic area protection. (`easy-mode`)
72. **Quick brush sizes** — Fine, Small, Medium, Wide, and Big are one tap away. (`quick-sizes`)
73. **Exact brush size** — Enter any whole-pixel size from 1 to 240. (`numeric-size`)
74. **Brush hardness** — Choose a crisp or feathered edge. (`hardness`)
75. **Square brush tip** — Paint with a square stamp. (`square-tip`)
76. **Chisel brush tip** — Use a flat angled tip for lettering and broad edges. (`chisel-tip`)
77. **Star brush** — Paint a trail of stars. (`star-tip`)
78. **Heart brush** — Paint a trail of hearts. (`heart-tip`)
79. **Dash brush** — Make short repeated marks. (`dash-tip`)
80. **Brush spacing** — Choose continuous paint or separated stamps. (`spacing`)
81. **Brush angle** — Turn a shaped tip before painting. (`tip-angle`)
82. **Tip roundness** — Squeeze a brush into a narrow oval. (`roundness`)
83. **Paint flow** — Build color with lighter or stronger stamps. (`flow`)
84. **Scattered paint** — Scatter stamps around the stroke path. (`scatter`)
85. **Rainbow brush** — Change color along the stroke. (`rainbow`)
86. **Pressure opacity** — Use a compatible pen to vary paint strength. (`pressure-opacity`)
87. **Live brush preview** — See a sample stroke before touching the page. (`tip-preview`)
88. **Personal brush preset** — Keep and restore a brush tip during this session. (`brush-preset`)
89. **Brush size shortcuts** — Use [ and ] for precise size changes. (`size-shortcuts`)
90. **Swap paint colors** — Exchange the current and secondary color. (`swap-colors`)
91. **Black & white reset** — Return to a classic outline palette. (`reset-colors`)
92. **Rectangle selection** — Drag a rectangular area. (`box-selection`)
93. **Ellipse selection** — Drag a circular or oval area. (`oval-selection`)
94. **Freehand selection** — Draw your own selection boundary. (`lasso-selection`)
95. **Add to a selection** — Combine separate selected areas. (`selection-add`)
96. **Subtract from a selection** — Protect a smaller part within the selection. (`selection-subtract`)
97. **Keep overlapping areas** — Restrict the selection to its intersection. (`selection-intersect`)
98. **Invert a selection** — Select everything around the current area. (`selection-invert`)
99. **Grow selection edges** — Expand a selection by a chosen distance. (`selection-grow`)
100. **Shrink selection edges** — Pull the boundary inward. (`selection-shrink`)
101. **Select the whole page** — Apply an operation across the drawing area. (`select-all`)
102. **Select existing paint** — Build a selection from the active layer’s paint. (`select-paint`)
103. **Reusable session selection** — Keep a selection and restore it later in this session. (`selection-keep`)
104. **Fill a custom selection** — Apply a solid color, gradient, or pattern. (`selection-fill`)
105. **Erase inside a selection** — Remove selected paint without changing the outline. (`selection-erase`)
106. **Move a selection** — Nudge it in four directions by a chosen distance. (`selection-move`)
107. **Hide selection tint** — Inspect the artwork without the selection overlay. (`selection-hide`)
108. **Automatic area protection** — Start a brush in a closed area to keep paint inside it. (`auto-inside`)
109. **Outline tap assistance** — Rescue a near-edge tap by finding a nearby open pixel. (`tap-assist`)
110. **Fill hand-drawn outlines** — Use dark drawn lines and outlined shapes as boundaries. (`drawn-boundaries`)
111. **Fill edge overlap** — Extend color slightly beneath preserved line art. (`fill-overlap`)
112. **Protect unpainted paper** — Constrain new painting to existing paint on the active layer. (`alpha-lock`)
113. **Clip a shading layer** — Show paint only where the lower visible paint layer has paint. (`clip-below`)
114. **RGB color entry** — Choose a color with red, green, and blue values. (`rgb-entry`)
115. **HSL color entry** — Choose hue, saturation, and lightness. (`hsl-entry`)
116. **Eyedropper averaging** — Sample one pixel or a 3×3 or 5×5 area. (`picker-average`)
117. **Palette from your artwork** — Keep dominant painted colors as swatches. (`artwork-palette`)
118. **Import a color palette** — Open a bounded JSON palette of up to 24 colors. (`palette-import`)
119. **Export a color palette** — Download your colors for reuse. (`palette-export`)
120. **Lighter color** — Create a lighter version of the current color. (`lighter-color`)
121. **Darker color** — Create a darker version of the current color. (`darker-color`)
122. **More vivid color** — Increase the saturation of the current color. (`vivid-color`)
123. **Shift color hue** — Explore a neighboring hue. (`hue-shift`)
124. **Grayscale check** — Inspect tonal contrast while keeping exports in color. (`grayscale-view`)
125. **Mirror the workspace** — Review proportions in a mirrored view; painting follows the view. (`mirror-view`)
126. **Rule of thirds guide** — Use composition guides that stay out of downloads. (`thirds-guide`)
127. **Rotate a download** — Export at 0°, 90°, 180°, or 270°. (`rotate-download`)
128. **Find a Studio starter** — Search 1,000 scene compositions by subject and scene. (`starter-search`)
129. **Starter detail filter** — Choose Easy, Medium, Hard, or Very Hard compositions. (`starter-detail`)
130. **Starter subject filter** — Browse animals, plants, vehicles, space, and food. (`starter-category`)
